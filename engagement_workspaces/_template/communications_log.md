# Communications Log — [ENGAGEMENT CODE]

Brief log of substantive consultant-client communications. Not
every email — only the substantive interactions that affect
engagement direction, methodology decisions, or scope.

The purpose is institutional memory: a second consultant joining
the engagement should be able to read this log and quickly
understand the engagement's recent history.

---

## Conventions

- One entry per substantive interaction
- Entries are reverse-chronological (newest at top)
- Each entry: date, channel, attendees/participants, summary,
  outcomes
- Outcomes link to follow-up items in `action_items.md` when
  applicable
- Routine email exchange (status pings, scheduling, simple
  acknowledgments) does NOT need a log entry

---

## What counts as substantive

- Quarterly review sessions
- Out-of-cycle methodology consultation
- Scope or contract conversations
- Client-side personnel transitions affecting the engagement
- Methodology version rollout coordination
- Escalations (technical issues, policy questions, regulatory
  developments affecting the client)
- New-finding triage discussions outside review windows
