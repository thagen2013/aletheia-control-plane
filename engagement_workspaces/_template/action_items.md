## Conventions

- Item IDs are sequential per workspace: A001, A002, ...
- "Owed by" identifies the responsible party (consultant, client,
  or shared)
- "Owed to" identifies the receiving party (the entity that benefits
  from completion)
- "Due date" can be a specific date or "next review" — most items
  cycle quarterly with the review cadence
- Action items are tracked at coarse granularity. Detailed
  remediation tasks for findings live in the deliverable bundle's
  `03_remediation_roadmap.md`, not here.
